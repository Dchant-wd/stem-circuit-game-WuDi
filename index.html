Short Description of the Game

Circuit Crafter is an interactive orbital engineering simulator. Players take the role of an avionics engineer tasked with restoring degraded power subsystems on an orbital research station. Players manipulate physical circuit parameters (series and parallel resistance) using Ohm's Law and configure digital logic gate switches (AND, OR, NOT) to balance current flow, avoid overload, and safely trigger critical relay switches.

Learning Objectives

  • Objective 1: Apply Ohm's Law ($I = V / R$) to calculate and balance target electric currents across series ($R_{eq} = R_1 + R_2$) and parallel ($1/R_{eq} = 1/R_1 + 1/R_2$) resistive networks.
  • Objective 2: Analyze and synthesize Boolean logic operations (AND, OR, NOT) to create truth-table configurations that successfully trigger protective hardware relays.
