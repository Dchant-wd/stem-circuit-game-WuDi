3. Game Description

Circuit Crafter: Logic & Current is a browser-based puzzle and simulation lab where players complete circuit loops, balance series and parallel resistances, and configure fundamental logic gates (AND, OR, NOT) to power critical space station subsystems without blowing high-voltage fuses or browning out auxiliary systems.

4. Learning Objectives
  • 1 Apply Ohm's Law and Circuit Equivalence: Quantitatively calculate and balance series and parallel resistance ($V = I \cdot R$) to regulate current within specific safe milliampere (mA) operational limits.
  • 2 Analyze and Predict Digital Logic States: Understand and evaluate truth table outcomes for basic digital logic gates (AND, OR, NOT) to execute fail-safe conditional power delivery.
